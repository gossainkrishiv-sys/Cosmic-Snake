# Cosmic Snake – Minimalist Arcade

Experience the classic Snake game with a clean, minimalist black-and-orange design.

Eat, grow, and beat your high score in a simple yet challenging arcade experience. Choose between two game modes, discover special magnetic food, and test your reflexes as the snake gets longer.

### GAME FEATURES

* **Classic Mode:** Avoid walls and don't collide with yourself.
* **No Walls Mode:** Pass through walls and appear on the opposite side.
* **Magnetic Food:** Discover special food that moves towards the snake.
* **Minimalist Design:** Clean black background with subtle dots and orange visuals.
* **Smooth Animations:** Enjoy glowing effects, fading segments, and particle animations.
* **Score Tracking:** Challenge yourself to beat your personal best.
* **Easy Controls:** Play using simple swipe or keyboard controls.

### TWO MODES. ONE GOAL.

Whether you prefer traditional Snake gameplay or the freedom of No Walls mode, Cosmic Snake offers a simple arcade challenge you can enjoy anytime.

**Play. Grow. Survive. Beat your score.**
